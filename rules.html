State Law & Governance

The State 266 Concordat

The binding constitutional codex established and enforced by [AVM], [SOK], and [ORA]. Ensuring domestic stability, fair resource access, and total state readiness for SvS.

📜 NAP-3 Enforced 🌾 Zero Tile Hitting 👑 Fair Castle Rotation ⚔️ Unified SvS Command
ARTICLE I • THE NON-AGGRESSION PACT (NAP-3)
STRICTLY ENFORCED

1.1 Mutual Peace Between Top Coalitions:

All members carrying the alliance tags [AVM], [SOK], or [ORA] are protected by an absolute Non-Aggression Pact. Unprovoked attacks, scouting runs with aggressive intent, rallies, or city burns against any member belonging to these alliances are strictly forbidden under all circumstances.

1.2 Alliance Territorial Boundaries:

Each alliance's Headquarters (HQ) and banner territories are sovereign. No alliance may place banners that intentionally sever or encircle another signatory's expansion path. Resource nodes located within an alliance's bordered territory belong primarily to that alliance's citizens.

ARTICLE II • RESOURCE GATHERING & TILE PROTECTION
ZERO TOLERANCE

2.1 Zero Tile Hitting:

Resource gathering nodes (Meat, Wood, Coal, Iron) across all provinces are 100% sacred for all players in State 266. Attacking a player while they are gathering on a resource tile is treated as an act of war against the state.

2.2 March Collision Etiquette:

In the event that two players march to the same unoccupied node simultaneously, the player whose march departed second or arrives second is obligated to immediately recall their troops. If an accidental tile hit occurs due to latency, the offending player must apologize in chat and compensate any lost resources.

ARTICLE III • SUNFIRE CASTLE & PRESIDENCY ROTATION
DEMOCRATIC CONSENSUS

3.1 Alternating Presidency:

The control of Sunfire Castle and the appointment of the State President rotates fairly between [AVM], [SOK], and [ORA]. This guarantees that all three alliances share the benefits of the Presidency, castle buffs, and honorary reward chests.

3.2 24/7 Minister Title Service:

The reigning President must maintain active title distributors to grant Minister buffs (Interior for Construction speed, Strategy for Research speed, Defense for Training speed) to any citizen of State 266 upon request. Maximum title holding duration is 15 minutes during busy periods to ensure smooth queue turnover.

ARTICLE IV • BROTHERS IN ARMS / KILL EVENT (KE) PROTOCOL
RESTRICTED COMBAT

4.1 NAP Protection Remains Absolute:

During the regular bi-weekly 'Brothers in Arms' event, NAP-3 protections remain fully active. Members of AVM, SOK, and ORA may not attack each other under the pretext of earning event points.

4.2 Safe Farming Targets:

Players may only hit untagged, inactive farm cities with prior scout verification. Multiple consecutive hits that exceed hospital capacity and result in permanent troop deaths are strictly banned.

ARTICLE V • STATE VS STATE (SvS) WAR MOBILIZATION
TOTAL WAR PROTOCOL

5.1 Unified State Council:

When State 266 is matched against an enemy server in the State vs State (SvS) tournament, all domestic rivalries are suspended. The leaders and seasoned veterans (including Endeavour, TrulyBee, and LululaKastagne) form the unified State War Council.

5.2 Turret & Castle Defense:

All 3 alliances coordinate garrison shifts to hold the Sunfire Castle and 4 Sunfire Turrets. Players with the highest Lethality and Health hero gear take point as garrison captains. Lower-power players reinforce strictly with tier-appropriate troops under captain command.

ARTICLE VI • ARBITRATION & ROGUE DESIGNATION
DISCIPLINARY CODES

6.1 Resolution Procedure:

1. Direct Settlement: Contact the player or their alliance R4 officer directly with battle report screenshots.
2. Council Hearing: If unresolved, present evidence to the State 266 Council.
3. Rogue Outlaw Status: Any player who repeatedly attacks tiles, burns NAP cities, or refuses council rulings will be formally declared a State Rogue. Rogues forfeit all NAP protection and are zeroed on sight by all 3 alliances.
