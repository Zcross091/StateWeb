Bear Trap Combat

Bear Trap Multiplier Formula: Max Damage Breakdown

The difference between 20M damage and 150M+ damage in Bear Trap isn't just power—it is rally leader buff stacking and joiner hero selection.

  • Joiner Heroes: Only send Jessie (first skill gives up to +25% total damage), Jeronimo (+15% attack), Seo-yoon (+10% attack), or Mia (+10% damage).
  • Defensive Mistake: NEVER send Sergey, Flint, or Patrick as joiner leads—their skills buff HP/DEF, adding ZERO damage to the bear!
  • Troop Ratio: Optimal layout is 10% Infantry, 10% Lancer, 80% Marksman (or 20/20/60 if your Marksmen are depleted).
  • Leader Rotation: Ensure your top 4 rally callers have the highest Lethality gear and maxed Chief combat skills.
Furnace Evolution

Fire Crystal Roadmap: From FC1 to FC10

Fire Crystals are the scarcest commodity after reaching Furnace 30. How you allocate crystals dictates your progression speed and combat survivability.

  • Priority 1 - Furnace: Always complete the Furnace first to unlock max troop capacity and the next camp tier.
  • Priority 2 - Camps: Infantry Camp first (unbreakable meatshields), followed by Marksman Camp (main DPS), then Lancer Camp.
  • Infirmary & Tech: Upgrade auxiliary buildings only after core combat camps reach parity.
  • SvS Hoarding: Never press upgrade buttons during regular peacetime weeks—hoard Fire Crystals for SvS Preparation Phase Day 4/5.
State vs State

SvS Prep Phase: How to Score 10M+ Points

Winning SvS begins 6 days prior to the Sunfire castle conquest. The preparation leaderboard determines castle buffs and state morale.

  • Day 1 - Chief Gear & Charms: Save all Hardened Alloy and Polishing Solution exclusively for this 24-hour cycle.
  • Day 2 - Hero Shards: Hoard general mythic shards, hero recruitment keys, and arena rewards for Day 2.
  • Day 3 - Pet Enhancements: Save all pet food and evolution marks for maximum point multipliers.
  • Day 4 & 5 - Speedups: Request Minister of Interior (Construction +10%) or Defense (Training +10%) buffs before burning long speedups.
Team Building

Hero Synergy & Gear Formations (Gen 1 to Gen 5+)

Hero gear stat priority: Lethality > Health > Attack > Defense. How to build unbreakable frontlines and lethal rally leads.

  • Infantry Line: Sergey & Flint (early anchors) → Ahmose / Hector (late game). Prioritize Health & Defense gear on Infantry.
  • Lancer Line: Molly & Philly → Mia / Lynn. Prioritize Lethality and Attack gear.
  • Marksman Line: Alonso & Greg → Wayne / Reina. The primary DPS carry of every combat encounter.
  • Gear Mastery: Reforge hero gear with Mythic stones to unlock set bonuses (+15% rally lethality).
Defense Protocol

Zero-Loss City Defense & Hospital Management

How to safeguard your army during cross-state warfare and prevent permanent casualty death into the enlistment office.

  • 24h Shield Discipline: Activate a 24-hour peace shield before 00:00 UTC on SvS Battle day unless actively garrisoning.
  • 70% Hospital Buffer: Keep at least 30% of infirmary beds unoccupied so that unexpected hits never result in dead troops.
  • Reinforcement Etiquette: Never reinforce a teammate unless they have designated high-stat garrison captains.
  • Safe Ghost Rallies: If attacked without a shield, launch 8-hour rallies on distant Barbarian Camps to protect troops.
Economic Growth

Economy & Farm Account Architecture

Mastering resource balance: Steel, Coal, Meat, and Wood. How top players run farm accounts to fuel endless high-tier upgrades.

  • Farm City Positioning: Cluster farm accounts near alliance border territories for fast, safe gathering marches.
  • Research Specialization: Maximize Growth Tech trees first (Engineering and Mathematics) before branching to military.
  • Arena of Glory: Complete 5 daily battles every single day to purchase essential Hero Gear Enhancement Stones.
Barbarian Defense

Crazy Joe Defense: Max Point Strategy

How to place in the top 3 on the state leaderboard during the 20-wave Crazy Joe barbarian assault.

  • Empty Your City: Send all your troops out to reinforce online and offline alliance members.
  • HQ Garrison: Station a high-capacity defense lead in the Alliance HQ during Waves 10, 14, and 20.
  • Hero Deployment: Send Patrick and Sergey to teammates' cities to grant them defensive recovery buffs.
