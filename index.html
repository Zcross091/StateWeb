State 266 Concordat & NAP

Binding state treaties established by AVM, SOK, and ORA to guarantee domestic stability, fair resource access, and overwhelming SvS triumph.

🛡️

1. NAP-3 Non-Aggression Pact

Strict non-aggression among the top 3 alliances:

  • No city attacks: Raiding member cities of AVM, SOK, or ORA is strictly prohibited.
  • Zero tile hitting: Gathering resource nodes across all provinces are 100% sacred for all citizens.
  • Banner & HQ integrity: Alliance territory boundaries are respected without encroaching builds.
👑

2. Sunfire Castle & Presidency

Sunfire Castle governance is managed by state consensus:

  • Rotational Presidency: The Presidency alternates fairly between top alliances to share state buffs.
  • State Title Allocation: Minister titles (Construction, Research, Training) are distributed on-demand 24/7.
  • Honorary Awards: Castle reward packs distributed based on SvS and event contributions.
⚔️

3. Brothers In Arms / KE Protocol

Strict rules during the Brothers In Arms (Kill Event):

  • NAP tags safe: AVM, SOK, and ORA members are forbidden from hitting each other during KE.
  • Unshielded inactive farm hits only: Only unaligned, untagged inactive cities may be scavenged.
  • Hospital limit discipline: Never cause permanent troop loss to State 266 survivors.
🔥

4. SvS Total Mobilization

During State vs State (SvS) War Phase:

  • Unified State Command: AVM, SOK, and ORA leaders form the State War Council.
  • Sunfire Defense: Coordinated garrison rotations and turret defense against enemy invaders.
  • Invading Rallies: Synchronized double-rallies led by our highest lethality captains.

Veteran Knowledge Vault

Endeavour's Survival Academy

Curated strategies distilled from 1+ year of coaching newbies and serving as a Whiteout Survival Discord Mod. Built to turn fresh survivors into state champions.

⚔️ Combat Mastery

Bear Trap: Max Damage & Rally Multipliers

The secret to cracking top damage scores in Bear Trap isn't just troop count—it is rally leader buff stacking and joiner hero selection.

  • Joiner Heroes: Only send Jessie (up to +25% damage boost), Jeronimo, Seo-yoon, or Mia. Never send defensive heroes as rally joiners!
  • Troop Ratio: 10% Infantry, 10% Lancer, 80% Marksman (or 20/20/60 depending on your gear).
  • Chief Skills: Activate 'Immediate Production' and combat attack buffs right before the trap opens.
🔥 Furnace Progression

Fire Crystal (FC) Roadmap: FC1 to FC5+

Fire Crystals are the scarcest commodity after reaching Furnace 30. How you allocate them dictates your survival in State 266 and SvS.

  • Priority 1 - Furnace: Always upgrade your Furnace first to unlock max troop capacity and camp tiers.
  • Priority 2 - Camps: Infantry Camp first (stronger meatshields), then Marksman, then Lancer.
  • Save for SvS: Never push FC upgrades during peacetime lull days—hoard crystals for SvS Preparation Phase Day 4/5.
🏆 State vs State

SvS Prep Phase: How to Score 10M+ Points

Winning State vs State begins 6 days before the Sunfire battle. The preparation phase leaderboard determines who controls castle bonuses.

  • Day 1 - Chief Gear & Charms: Hoard polish tools and alloy. Only upgrade during this 24h window.
  • Day 2 - Hero Shards: Never use general mythic shards until Day 2.
  • Day 4 & 5 - Speedups: Coordinate your biggest construction/training speedups with Minister titles and alliance pet buffs.
🛡️ Team Building

Hero Formations & Gear Optimization

Optimizing hero gear stats: Lethality, Health, Defense, and Attack. How to assemble unbeatable rally lines.

  • Infantry: Sergey & Flint (F2P/Low Spender anchors) -> Ahmose / Hector in later generations. Prioritize Health & Defense gear.
  • Lancers: Molly & Philly -> Mia. Prioritize Lethality & Attack gear.
  • Marksmen: Alonso & Greg -> Lynn. The primary DPS carry of every combat encounter.
🛡️ Defense Protocol

City Defense & Zero-Loss Hospital Management

How to safeguard your troops during hostile cross-state incursions and prevent catastrophic casualties.

  • Shield Schedules: 24h shields active prior to 00:00 UTC on SvS Battle day.
  • Garrison Buffs: Never reinforce a burning city unless you have designated high-stat garrison leads.
  • Hospital Overflow: Keep infirmary capacity above 70% free to prevent troop death into enlistment offices.
💰 Economic Growth

Economy & Farm Account Architecture

Mastering resource balance: Steel, Coal, Meat, and Wood. How top players run farm accounts to fuel constant upgrades.

  • Farm Alt Placement: Keep farm cities near alliance borders for safe gathering.
  • Research Specialization: Maximize Growth Tech trees first (Engineering and Mathematics).
  • Arena of Glory: Daily 5 attacks for store currency to buy hero gear stones.
